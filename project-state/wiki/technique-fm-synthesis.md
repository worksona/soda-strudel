# FM synthesis

Part of [[repertoire-techniques]] · [[repertoire]].

Frequency modulation gives bells, electric pianos and growling basses from one oscillator: fm sets the modulation amount, fmh the harmonicity ratio, and envelopes shape how the tone changes over a note.

**Strudel docs:** https://strudel.cc/learn/synths/#fm-synthesis
