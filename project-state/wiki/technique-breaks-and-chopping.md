# Breaks: chopping, slicing and re-sequencing

Part of [[repertoire-techniques]] · [[repertoire]].

Breakbeat music (jungle, drum & bass, breakcore) cuts a drum loop into pieces and plays them back in a new order: chop, slice, splice and striate cut a sample; fit and loopAt stretch it to the cycle.

**Strudel docs:** https://strudel.cc/learn/samples/#sampler-effects
