# Sound banks

GitHub sample banks for Strudel (`samples('github:owner/repo')`), from awesome-strudel. Part of [[repertoire]]. **Rights-checked 2026-11-01** against [[repertoire-reuse-and-citation]] (names, READMEs, licences – no listening yet). Shipping waits on a listening pass (backlog). Credits: [[repertoire-credits]].

## ✅ usable with credit (3)

- **[AuditeMarlow/samples](https://github.com/AuditeMarlow/samples)** (`REP-B-002`) – MIT – 3 sounds: hihat, kick, snare
  - MIT, author states they made the samples; generic drum one-shot names, nothing suggests sampled records.
  - Load: `samples('github:AuditeMarlow/samples')`
  - Credit: Drum samples by Audite Marlow (Niek den Breeje) — https://github.com/AuditeMarlow/samples — MIT
- **[fstiffo/polifonia-samples](https://github.com/fstiffo/polifonia-samples)** (`REP-B-011`) – CC0-1.0 – 1 sounds: italy
  - CC0, contents are individual church-bell notes with heritage documentation; no commercial recordings. Repo branch is master, so the github: shortcut needs the branch.
  - Load: `samples('github:fstiffo/polifonia-samples/master')`
  - Credit: Bell samples (San Pietro, Avegno) by Francesco Stiffoni / Polifonia Song Contest 2024 — https://github.com/fstiffo/polifonia-samples — CC0 1.0
- **[hvillase/cavlp-25p](https://github.com/hvillase/cavlp-25p)** (`REP-B-012`) – GPL-3.0 – 3 sounds: botella, pelota, spray
  - GPL-3.0 and contents are clearly foley of household objects; nothing from records. Note GPL on audio requires keeping licence notice/credit.
  - Load: `samples('github:hvillase/cavlp-25p')`
  - Credit: Object sounds by Hugo Villaseñor (hvillase), course cavlp-25p — https://github.com/hvillase/cavlp-25p — GPL-3.0

## ✉️ ask the owner (4)

- **[emrexdeger/strudelSamples](https://github.com/emrexdeger/strudelSamples)** (`REP-B-009`) – CC0-1.0 – 15 sounds: k, s, ch, opal, b, clap, north, oh
  - CC0 declared and names are generic, but no origin is stated — drum one-shots/pads may come from third-party sample packs whose licences forbid redistribution; owner should listen to north/opal/scape/wt_my and ideally ask the author.
- **[fjpolo/fjpolo-Strudel](https://github.com/fjpolo/fjpolo-Strudel)** (`REP-B-010`) – MIT (repo); samples are Freesound under their own licences – 3 sounds: anvil, chains, metalgrinder
  - Loading the bank pulls in a CC BY-NC sound (anvil:1) and needs per-sound CC-BY credits, not just 'MIT'; chains is a 51 MB file. Could re-host the CC-BY/CC0 subset with per-sound credit instead.
