# Chiptune voices: square, triangle, pulse and noise

Part of [[repertoire-techniques]] · [[repertoire]].

Game-console music uses a few simple waveforms: square/pulse for leads, triangle for bass, white or pink noise for drums. In Strudel these are sound names (square, triangle, pulse, sqr, tri, white, pink) shaped with short envelopes, crush and clip.

**Strudel docs:** https://strudel.cc/learn/synths/
