# Arrangement: building a song from sections

Part of [[repertoire-techniques]] · [[repertoire]].

A Strudel piece is a loop until you give it a plan. arrange() plays patterns for a set number of cycles each, in order; struct, mask and when switch parts on and off inside a pattern; pick chooses between parts by a pattern of names or numbers.

**Strudel docs:** https://strudel.cc/learn/conditional-modifiers/
