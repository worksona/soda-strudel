# Repertoire

soda.strudel's growing catalogue of Strudel songs, patterns, learning resources and sound banks – each one cited, audited and described. Research mandate: RES-M-001 (locked, standing, weekly). Records: `documents/repertoire/catalogue.yaml` (reviewed catalogue) and `research/candidates/` (458 longlist candidates from the first harvest, still to score). In the app: Examples › Repertoire.

**Catalogue: 52 songs · 17 learning resources and tools · 30 sound banks.** First harvest (2026-11-01): 5 research steps, 40 findings, 458 candidates, 35 new sources – see [[repertoire-harvest-2026-11-01]].

**Reuse policy decided 2026-11-01:** adapt Felix Roos's CC BY-NC-SA tunes while soda.strudel is non-commercial; unlicensed pieces are linked, adapted only on the author's written yes; only open-licensed sound banks; our research indexes and links only. Details: [[repertoire-reuse-and-citation]].

## Pipeline
inventoried → audited (link, licence, date) → described → catalogued → approved on the Queue (catalogue review cards) → used in Learn / Examples (a build task, only where the licence allows). New finds enter as longlist candidates and are scored before they join the catalogue.
