# Chords, scales and voicings

Part of [[repertoire-techniques]] · [[repertoire]].

Harmony without music theory overload: scale turns numbers into notes of a key; chord symbols plus voicing (with dict) pick playable chord shapes; scaleTranspose and add move lines inside the key.

**Strudel docs:** https://strudel.cc/understand/voicings/
